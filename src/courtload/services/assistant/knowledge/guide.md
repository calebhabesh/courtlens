# CourtLoad guide

## players | Find and compare players | /stats,/compare | player,players,compare,comparison,find,search
Use player search to select a named player. In Stats, add players to the comparison tray and open Compare. Keep the selected season, phase, cutoff and window when comparing. Team badges describe observed participation in that scope, rather than a current roster.

## teams | Find and compare teams | /teams,/team-compare | team,teams
Open Teams to inspect observed team outcomes, then select named teams in Team Compare. Team records describe admitted finalized outcomes; record ties alone do not establish official seeds.

## wowy | With or without you | /wowy | wowy,on-off,with,without
WOWY means “with or without you”: it compares exposure with selected players together or apart. Shared court time needs admitted lineups and possessions. Whole-game participation answers a different question; an absent box-score row does not prove injury or absence.

## scope | Seasons, phases and windows | /stats | season,phase,window,cutoff
Regular season and playoffs are separate scopes. A cutoff excludes games on that date and all later dates. Recent windows use observed appearances, rather than calendar days. Changing the scope can change the eligible cohort and sample.

## metrics | Common metrics | /definitions | metric,definition,ppg,apg,assist,assists,true,shooting,effective,percentage
PPG and APG are points and assists per valid appearance. Shooting percentages use summed makes and attempts, rather than averaging game percentages. Effective field-goal percentage accounts for the extra value of made shots from beyond the arc. True shooting uses points relative to shooting opportunities with the declared free-throw approximation. A research sample filter is separate from NBA leader qualification.

## forecasts | Forecasts and descriptive results | /forecasts,/models | forecast,prediction,interval,descriptive
Descriptive results summarize admitted observations. Registered points forecasts are conditional on appearing; their intervals describe calibrated empirical dispersion. Overlapping intervals do not establish a winner probability or an automatic start/sit decision.

## saved | Saved findings | /saved | save,saved,finding,export
Save a verified analytical finding explicitly to retain its immutable inputs and result identity. Open it in Saved to reproduce or export it. Conversation history is separate from analytical findings; deleting a chat must not delete a saved finding.

## settings | Settings tasks | /settings | settings,portrait,photos,configuration,key
Open Settings to change portraits and workspace preferences or inspect data and assistant configuration. A configured provider key does not prove inference health. Core manual analysis works without a provider.

## pick_roll | Pick and roll | /learning | pick,roll,screen
A pick and roll is an action where a teammate screens the ball handler’s defender, then moves toward the basket. The ball handler can drive, pass to the rolling teammate or find another open player. Defensive responses include switching, dropping and showing help; the best choice depends on personnel and spacing.

## defense | Discussing defense | /definitions | defender,defense,defensive
For a defender comparison, I would weigh point-of-attack resistance, help positioning, rim protection, screen navigation and role. Steals and blocks describe particular events and do not by themselves measure overall defensive quality. A player-specific judgment needs supported examples and an explicit choice of criteria.


## switching | Switching on defense | /learning | switching,switch
Switching on defense means defenders exchange assignments when an offensive screen or movement changes the matchup. It can limit open lanes, but may leave a defender guarding a less favorable matchup.

## spacing | Offensive spacing | /learning | spacing,offense
Spacing means distributing offensive players to create space for drives, passes and shots. Defenders must cover that space, so a well-spaced offense can make help defense more difficult.

## offensive_rebounding | Offensive rebounding | /definitions | rebounding,rebound,offensive
Offensive rebounding means recovering a missed shot by your own team. It extends the possession and can create another scoring opportunity, while committing players to the glass can leave less protection against a fast break.

## pace | Pace | /definitions | pace,possession
Pace describes how frequently possessions occur over a standardized amount of playing time. A faster pace creates more opportunities for counting statistics without necessarily indicating greater efficiency.

## usage | Usage rate | /definitions | usage,possession
Usage rate describes the share of team possessions a player finishes through shot attempts, free-throw trips and turnovers while on the court. It describes offensive involvement rather than passing volume or overall player quality; definitions depend on the declared possession approximation.

## ball_handling | Ball handling rules | /learning | traveling,travelling,dribble
Traveling is moving illegally with the ball while holding it, subject to the gather and pivot rules. A double dribble occurs when a player ends a dribble and starts dribbling again without a permitted intervening event.

## zone | Zone defense | /learning | zone
Zone defense assigns defenders to areas and passing lanes, with responsibilities that shift as the ball and offensive players move. Defenders still track nearby threats and communicate coverage rather than staying fixed in place.


## assist_definition | Assists | /definitions | assist
An assist is a pass that directly leads to a teammate making a basket, subject to the scorer’s judgment about the immediate scoring action. It credits the passer for creating the scoring opportunity rather than every pass during a possession.
