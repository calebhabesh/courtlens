# B29 financial query operations v1

`POST /api/workbench/operations/finance` consumes `FinanceQuery` and returns `FinanceResult`; both appear in OpenAPI. This read-only operation is shared by manual clients and future tools. It requires an explicit immutable B28 book UUID, consecutive financial season, timezone-aware `known_at` and inclusive `effective_on`. It does not choose a newest branch or use a basketball season, roster, metric or rating as financial evidence. No database migration, acquisition, publication or worker job runs on a query.

## Dates and evidence

Selected book membership is resolved at `known_at`: when a selected correction was observed later, follow its retained `supersedes_id` to the observation actually available then. Do not resurrect sibling branch observations. Membership is bounded to 5,000 rows; correction traversal allows at most 100 levels / 50,000 observations. Missing/cyclic/over-budget relationships fail explicitly. Effective dates after `effective_on` do not enter contract calculations. Unknown effective dates default to `include_labelled`: display them as unresolved reports, but exclude seasonal money from subtotals. `exclude` removes these observations from the contract calculation. No acquisition date becomes a historical announcement/effective date.

Contract calculations include only the selected financial season and seasonless terms. The timeline for returned contracts retains all known-at selected season observations, including explicitly labelled future effective rows, guarantees, option observations and unresolved dates. It does not treat option seasons as realized outcomes. Timeline rows excluded from calculations are still source reports; the request's scenario assumption does not rewrite them. `historical_reconstruction` identifies information acquired after the requested effective date. An empty historical result means the book was not yet known, not that its players were unsigned.

Each observation carries its original book, source metadata/URL/hash/locator, original terms, content hash and correction target/reason. Inherited sources are resolved against the origin book, never only the descendant manifest. Source document bytes are not loaded by the query. The complete response is reproducible with `result_sha256`; `analytical_content_sha256` separately hashes eligible numerical context, terms and provenance without installation/observation/book UUIDs or unrelated future timeline rows. Same original eligible facts in an as-of descendant reproduce the analytical hash even though their book-bound response hashes differ.

## Arithmetic, coverage and scenarios

USD cents are integer arithmetic internally and decimal integer **strings** on the response wire, preserving large aggregates in JavaScript. No valid amounts yields null; an explicitly known zero yields `"0"`. One amount kind is selected: base salary, cash, cap hit, cap hold, dead money or tax component. Contract total, guaranteed total, AAV and unspecified amounts cannot enter seasonal payroll. Reported total / documented years can produce a separately labelled derived AAV in decimal USD at 40 significant digits; it never produces a seasonal schedule. Missing total/year denominator yields null.

The expected denominator is the count of represented contract groups in the filtered source book, not a complete team roster. Known and unknown contract counts reconcile to it; player-ID sets can overlap when a player has multiple contracts. External expected roster identities remain unknown. Team association must be explicitly dated in eligible observations for that contract; contradictory teams are unresolved, and no association is inferred from participation. Team filters exclude unresolved associations.

Unexercised or unresolved player/team options make seasonal amounts conditional; declined options are excluded. `reported-v1` exposes only eligible reported amounts. The named `include-unexercised-options-v1` scenario assumes conditional options are exercised; scenario money is returned in separate contract/subtotal fields, and the observed known subtotal stays unchanged. Unknown salary or effective date never becomes scenario money. Guarantees remain separate source terms; no guaranteed schedule is synthesized from a contract-wide total.

Small vs Big uses the eligible **reported** amount for the selected kind, default $12M, with equality in Big. Unknown/conditional amounts remain Unknown. The caller can select the integer-cent threshold; it is a grouping parameter, not an official financial threshold. Position allocation currently keeps every known cent in `unclassified`: no dated, book-bound position inputs have been admitted here. Official cap/tax/apron values, versioned legal component rules and roster completeness are absent, so every result returns `threshold_status_pending` and cannot claim exact room/tax/compliance. Modeled value/surplus/ROI remains `source_blocked`; no dollar conversion from B25/B26 ratings is invented.

## Status denominator

Groups are exclusive per `(NBA player ID, contract ID)` in the requested season/source book, not per current free-agent player. One unambiguous documented status selects its category; absent/null or conflicting seasonal/seasonless statuses use `unknown_review`. Blank terms never imply unsigned. Status rows may be admitted extension announcements, explicitly not a current market classification. The six original B28 categories are retained. Unfiltered counts apply team/player/date/season filters before status selection; filtered counts and financial subtotals apply the requested status selection. Both group sets reconcile to their labelled contract counts.

## Verification and boundaries

Unit/HTTP fixtures reconcile cents, independent amount kinds, zero/nulls, totals/AAV, missing dates, season/future exclusions, inherited correction evidence, teams, status groups and option scenarios. Disposable PostgreSQL schemas run the B28 migration/sealing with B29 HTTP reads. `scripts/verify_b29_finance.py OUTPUT.json` exclusively writes evidence from the retained B28 books inside a read-only/repeatable-read PostgreSQL transaction and verifies retained bytes/memberships and publication pointers afterward. The measured sample supports a historical reported $120M / 3-year derived $40M AAV; seasonal salary remains unknown. This is in-process HTTP/database evidence, not browser or network-server acceptance.

B30 owns the UI and selection/navigation workflow. B35 owns generic saved-operation/export integration. Comprehensive seasonal salary/free-agent admission, dated position binding, official threshold rules, optional monetary model conversion and full fresh restore/browser acceptance retain separate gates.
