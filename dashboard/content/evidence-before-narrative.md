# Evidence before narrative

CourtLens engineering note · 2026-10-04 · Original project content

## What a saved investigation retains

A table can look convincing while losing the release, cutoff or sample that produced it. CourtLens retains the canonical plan, resolved context, operation versions, input identities, result hashes and exact evidence cells together. The question is optional metadata. AI-generated prose is excluded.

Saving reruns the selected server-held turn against pinned inputs before accepting it. Stats expands to the full eligible cohort within the 2,000-row bound. A distribution still means its requested historical window; similarity still means its declared dimensions and top-k. Neither becomes a complete career report.

[Run a manual investigation](/research/ask) and inspect [retained evidence](/investigations). The core workflow needs no Gemini key.

## Missingness and precision

Zero records an observed numerical outcome. Null records an unknown value with an explicit reason where available. An absent player box row proves neither a zero outcome nor an injury. Presentation rounds table decimals; exact evidence and JSON retain returned precision, including any upstream aggregation rounding.

The CSV export carries both exact evidence cells and chunked manifest JSON. It escapes formula-like strings. For large manifests and faithful types, use JSON. A screenshot is a presentation artifact, not a replacement for the analytical manifest.

## Reproduction under uncertainty

Reproduction checks the retained context and operation versions, re-executes the plan without AI, and compares results, evidence and destinations. A mismatch is visible. Unsupported versions remain exportable instead of silently adopting current semantics. Forecasts with a recorded issuance pin that issuance; an absence result cannot establish prospective accuracy.

[Methodology](/methodology), [forecast evidence](/forecasts) and [evaluation archive](/evaluation) explain the remaining boundaries. Overlapping empirical prediction intervals do not identify a winner or an automatic start/sit decision.

## Local evidence and provider records

Active conversation state expires after 30 minutes. Clearing it leaves explicitly saved investigations intact. A saved title and an opted-in question are durable database metadata. Google’s provider-held records have a separate lifecycle; a local clear action does not erase them.

[Settings](/settings) describes optional AI and installation. [Data status and operations](/data-status) reports source coverage and job state. Real Gemini language acceptance and deeper source/model gates remain open; retained-operation correctness alone does not close them.
